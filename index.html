Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Christmas List is a shared gift-planning app. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information and Google account access

When you choose to sign in, Christmas List uses Google OAuth to ask for permission to access Google Sheets. The app requests the Google Sheets permission needed to read and make changes to the spreadsheet configured for this app. Google handles the sign-in process.

The app does not ask for or use your Gmail, Google Drive files generally, contacts, calendar, or Google account profile information as part of its gift-list features.

Information stored in the spreadsheet

The app reads and writes the people and gift information in the connected Google Sheet. This may include names, gift names, gift status (planned or bought), optional notes, and record dates. Anyone who has been given access to that spreadsheet may be able to see or edit its contents.

How information is used and shared

Spreadsheet information is used only to display and manage the shared Christmas gift list. The app does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with the app owner through a separate database. The app sends requests directly from your browser to the Google Sheets API. Google processes those requests under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Tokens and storage

Google provides an access token to the app after you authorise it. The app uses that token in the browser to make authorised Sheets API requests. The app code does not deliberately save the access token in its own database or send it to an app-owner server. Google may use its own cookies and security mechanisms during sign-in.

Retention and deletion

Gift-list records remain in the connected Google Sheet until someone with spreadsheet access changes or deletes them. To remove information, edit or delete it in the spreadsheet or use the app's delete controls. You can also revoke the app's Google account access from your Google Account security settings.

Security

Access is controlled by Google sign-in and the sharing permissions on the spreadsheet. Only share the spreadsheet with people you trust. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. For questions about this app or its data, use the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
